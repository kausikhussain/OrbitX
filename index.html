Planetary Defense Standards

Potentially Hazardous Asteroid (PHA): Official classification for asteroids with Minimum Orbit Intersection Distance (MOID) $\le 0.05\text{ AU}$ ($\approx 7,480,000\text{ km}$) and absolute magnitude $H \le 22.0$ (roughly $\ge 140\text{ m}$). It implies close monitoring, not an inevitable collision.

Lunar Distance (LD): The mean distance from the center of Earth to the Moon ($384,400\text{ km}$).
